# Perigee

Perigee bottles the pull of the moon. Tide Basins condense brine at night, and each moon phase gives a different brine.

## Tide Basin

Fill a Tide Basin with a water bucket under open sky. Over half a night the water condenses into brine of tonight's tide: Full, Waning, New or Waxing. Each fill gives 3 bottles. Brine left in the basin through a sunny day dries into Phase Salt.

## Lenses

Set a Blank Lens on a Tidal Stand and pour 4 bottles of one brine over it to make that brine's lens. Pour 8 more of the same brine over a lens to fix it.

## Phase Medallion

The Phase Medallion goes in the necklace slot. Hold a lens in the other hand and use the medallion to set it.

- A Full Tide Lens lets the wearer walk on water.
- A Waxing Tide Lens draws dropped items and XP in.
- A New Tide Lens makes hostile mobs lose track of the wearer in shadow.
- A Waning Tide Lens spreads damage taken over a few seconds.

A lens is strongest on its own tide's nights and fades as the moon moves on. A fixed lens works at full strength every night.

## Special moons

The rare Perigee moon rises only on full moons. Basins fill twice as fast and make Perigee Brine, whose lens holds all four tide powers at half strength. Special moons from Fundamentum, Ostentum and Ars Moriendi each give their own brine and lens.

## Tide Dial

The Tide Dial gives no signal by day. At night it gives the pull of the moon phase, and full power on a special moon.
